Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  TKMaster Consulting and Systems

TKMaster Consulting and Systems respects the privacy of those who access this website and is committed to protecting your personal data, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018).

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and what your rights are as a data subject.

1. What data we collect

We collect data only when you voluntarily provide it or when it is generated by your browsing:

  • Contact and registration forms: name, email, phone, area of expertise and professional information you fill in.
  • Browsing: pages visited, session time and device type, collected via analytics cookies.
  • Communications: messages sent via WhatsApp or email to our team.

We do not collect sensitive data (racial origin, health, biometrics, etc.) and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

2. How we use your data

  • Respond to contacts and requests submitted by you.
  • Evaluate professional registrations for the talent pool.
  • Reach out when a compatible opportunity arises for your profile.
  • Improve the browsing experience based on aggregated and anonymous data.
  • Comply with legal obligations when required.

3. Cookies

This website uses cookies for basic functionality and traffic analysis. You can decline non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit or through your browser settings.

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how the site is used, without identifying you personally.

4. Data sharing

Your data is not sold. We may share it only:

  • With client companies, only when you, as a professional, are presented for an opportunity — and with your knowledge.
  • With technical service providers (hosting, email) who operate under our control and with a confidentiality obligation.
  • By legal requirement or court order.

5. How long we keep your data

Professional registration data is kept as long as there is mutual interest or until you request deletion. Contact data is kept for as long as necessary to fulfill your request. Browsing data follows the tool's policy, generally up to 26 months.

6. Your rights (LGPD)

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your data and access it.
  • Correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Request the deletion of your data.
  • Revoke consent at any time.
  • Request information about sharing with third parties.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The date of the last revision is always shown at the top of this page.
