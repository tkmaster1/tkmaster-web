FAQ

Questions every
professional asks

We've answered the most common ones here. If yours isn't listed, talk directly to us.

How does the process work after I register?

After registration, your profile enters TKMaster's talent pool. When a demand compatible with your profile — stack, seniority level and availability — comes up, we contact you directly, without intermediaries. You receive the opportunity description and decide whether to move forward.

How long until I'm contacted?

It depends on the availability of demands compatible with your profile. When there's an aligned opportunity, contact happens within 7 days. If there's nothing at the moment, your registration stays active and you're the first to know when something comes up.

Is there any cost to register?

No. Registration, process and referral are 100% free for the professional. TKMaster charges the client — never the placed professional.

Can I be on more than one project at the same time?

Yes, as long as it's technically feasible and agreed upon with all clients involved. TKMaster respects your actual availability — stated during registration — and won't refer the same professional to projects with incompatible workloads.

Do I need to be a PJ contractor to work with TKMaster?

In the current model, yes — placement is done via PJ contract. This means you need an active CNPJ to issue invoices. TKMaster guides you through the opening process if you don't have one yet. As the operation grows, other models may be considered.

Will I know what's happening during the placement?

Yes. Every professional placed by TKMaster has a dedicated Account Manager — your point of contact for any situation. We do regular check-ins, periodic reports and ensure you're never left without a response at any point during the project.

What happens when the project ends?

Our first response is to look for reallocation — not dismissal. When a project ends, the AM already starts mapping new opportunities in advance. A good professional doesn't sit idle: that's one of our core principles.

My stack isn't on the active demand list. Can I still register?

Yes. The talent pool is open to any IT profile. The list of stacks with active demand is dynamic — it changes as clients evolve. Your registration will already be in the database when a compatible opportunity comes up.
